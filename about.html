Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a simple goal: create a welcoming neighborhood bakery where people can enjoy handmade food and friendly service. Our daily work centers on fresh baking, dependable pre-orders, and a strong connection with the community we serve.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We choose ingredients with quality and consistency in mind and plan our menu around small-batch production. Seasonal offerings allow our bakers to highlight ingredients and flavors that fit the time of year while reducing unnecessary waste.

Meet Our Team

Our bakers prepare breads and pastries early each morning, while our front counter team helps customers with product questions, pickup details, and celebration orders. Everyone works together to create a warm and helpful bakery experience.
