Freshly Baked for Our Community

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery serving handmade breads, pastries, and celebration cakes. We welcome local families, commuters, and event planners looking for fresh baked goods made with care.

What We're Known For

Our bakery is known for crusty artisan breads, flaky morning pastries, and custom cakes prepared in small batches. We focus on warm service, dependable pre-orders, and flavors that bring people together.
